TrekTide Privacy Policy

Welcome to TrekTide, your trusted partner in bicycle adventures. We understand the importance of your privacy and are committed to safeguarding it. This Privacy Policy provides a detailed overview of how we collect, use, and protect your data. We strive to create a safe, transparent, and convenient experience for every customer. Whether you are using our website for information or renting a bike, you can rest assured that your data is in secure hands. By using our services, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy and trust us to ensure your safety.

Our Commitment to Privacy

At TrekTide, we believe protecting your privacy is an integral part of our service. We understand that every user deserves trust and transparency in data processing. We develop and implement state-of-the-art data protection methods to eliminate any risks. Our measures include using advanced encryption technologies, regular system security updates, and conducting frequent audits. These practices allow us to identify vulnerabilities and address them promptly. We aim to exceed your expectations by delivering services that not only meet but surpass industry standards for data security. Our goal is to build long-term relationships based on respect, confidentiality, and reliability.

Data Collection

To provide you with the most comfortable and personalized experience, TrekTide collects the minimum necessary information. We may request the following data:

  • Contact Information: Such as your name, email address, and phone number for account creation or order processing.
  • Payment Details: Required to process secure transactions through our trusted payment systems.
  • Rental Preferences: Information about the type of bike, rental duration, and pick-up/drop-off locations.
  • Website Usage Data: Statistics that help us improve site functionality and tailor services to your needs.

This data is collected only with your consent and is used solely for the services you request. We guarantee that your data will never be used for other purposes without your explicit consent. Your comfort and trust inspire us to continually improve our work.

Data Security

Protecting your data is our top priority. We have implemented a range of strict security measures to prevent unauthorized access, misuse, or data leaks. These measures include:

  • Data encryption to safeguard your payment and personal details.
  • Secure servers for data storage to prevent unauthorized usage.
  • Access controls ensuring only authorized personnel can handle your data.

Not only do we adhere to existing standards, but we actively explore new technologies and approaches to continuously enhance our security systems. Your data will never be sold or shared with third parties without your consent. If you have any questions or concerns about the security of your data, our team is always ready to assist. Contact us at: trektide@gmail.com.

Third-Party Services

To provide a full range of services, we may engage third-party providers, such as payment systems, marketing platforms, or analytical tools. We ensure that these providers strictly comply with our data protection requirements and use the information provided only to fulfill specific tasks. Before partnering with third parties, we thoroughly review their processes, technical capabilities, and compliance with legal requirements. Additionally, we regularly reassess their data protection practices to ensure they align with our standards. If a new platform is required to deliver a service, we will promptly inform you and provide comprehensive details about the type of data being processed and its intended purpose.

Location Services

As part of our service, we may request access to your location data. This is necessary to provide information about nearby rental points, route planning, or determining arrival times. This data is used solely to enhance your user experience and is not stored in our systems. You can always disable location access in your device settings if needed. We respect your right to privacy and aim to utilize technological capabilities to create a service that is both convenient and secure.

Children’s Privacy

TrekTide’s services are intended for users aged 18 and older. We understand the importance of protecting children’s privacy and take all necessary measures to avoid the accidental collection of their data. If we discover that information about a child has been collected, we will immediately delete it from our records. We encourage parents and guardians to closely monitor their children’s online activities and educate them about online safety. If you have any concerns regarding children’s privacy, you can contact us for further assistance.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, suggestions, or complaints regarding this Privacy Policy or data processing, please contact our team at: trektide@gmail.com. We strive to provide the most transparent and convenient interaction with our customers. Your feedback helps us improve and further strengthen our commitment to protecting your privacy. No matter the complexity of your question, we are ready to assist you promptly and professionally.
