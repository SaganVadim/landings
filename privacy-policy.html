Privacy Policy - Utensilium

Welcome to Utensilium's Privacy Policy. Your privacy is of utmost importance to us, and we are committed to safeguarding any personal information you share with us. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data when you visit our website or purchase products from our store. By using our site, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy, assuring you that we will handle your information responsibly and securely.

Please note that this Privacy Policy applies only to information collected directly through our website. It does not cover external websites or third-party services linked from our pages. We encourage you to review the privacy practices of these external sites before sharing any personal information. Staying informed about how your data is handled is crucial to maintaining your privacy.

Information We Collect

At Utensilium, we collect only the information necessary for providing our services. This may include your name, email address, shipping information, and payment details when you make a purchase or subscribe to our newsletter. We may also collect non-personal information, such as browsing behavior, to enhance your experience on our site. We do not collect sensitive information without your explicit consent.

Additionally, we may use cookies to improve your browsing experience. Cookies help us remember your preferences and make navigation more efficient. You can choose to disable cookies in your browser settings if you prefer not to use them. We strive to respect your choices and provide transparency regarding the data we collect.

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to process your orders, provide customer support, and keep you informed about new products and promotions related to Utensilium. We may also use your data to improve our website's functionality and enhance your experience. Marketing communications will only be sent with your permission.

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. The only exception is when we share information with trusted partners, like payment processors and shipping companies, to fulfill your orders. These partners are required to maintain strict confidentiality and use your data solely for completing transactions.

How We Secure Your Information

Keeping your information secure is a top priority for Utensilium. We employ industry-standard security measures, including encryption and secure servers, to protect your data from unauthorized access and misuse. Our team regularly reviews our security practices to stay current with the latest technologies and threats.

While we strive to protect your information, no online data transmission is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security. However, we are continually improving our protocols to ensure your data is as safe as possible.

Links to External Websites

For your convenience, our website may contain links to external websites, including social media platforms and third-party service providers. While we carefully select these links, Utensilium is not responsible for the content or privacy practices of these external sites. Each site may have its own privacy policy, and we recommend reviewing those policies before interacting with them.

Remember, once you leave our website, your interactions are governed by the privacy policies of the external site. We encourage you to be vigilant and understand how your information is collected and used on any site you visit.

Children’s Privacy

Utensilium is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware of any information collected from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete that information from our records.

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make updates, we will change the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. Any changes will take effect as soon as they are posted on our site.
