Privacy Policy - FootFinesse

Welcome to FootFinesse's Privacy Policy. Your privacy is of utmost importance to us, and we are committed to safeguarding any personal information you share with us. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data when you visit our website or book services with us. By using our site, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy, assuring you that we will handle your information responsibly and securely.

Please note that this Privacy Policy applies only to information collected directly through our website. It does not cover external websites or third-party services linked from our pages. We encourage you to review the privacy practices of these external sites before sharing any personal information.

Information We Collect

At FootFinesse, we collect only the information necessary for providing our services. This may include your name, email address, phone number, appointment details, and payment information when you book a service or contact us. We may also collect non-personal information, such as browsing behavior, to enhance your experience on our site.

Additionally, we may use cookies to improve your browsing experience. Cookies help us remember your preferences and make navigation more efficient. You can choose to disable cookies in your browser settings if you prefer not to use them.

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to process your bookings, provide customer support, and keep you informed about new services and promotions related to FootFinesse. Marketing communications will only be sent with your permission.

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. The only exception is when we share information with trusted partners, like payment processors, to fulfill your appointments.

How We Secure Your Information

Keeping your information secure is a top priority for FootFinesse. We employ industry-standard security measures, including encryption and secure servers, to protect your data from unauthorized access and misuse.

While we strive to protect your information, no online data transmission is completely secure. However, we are continually improving our protocols to ensure your data is as safe as possible.

Links to External Websites

For your convenience, our website may contain links to external websites, including social media platforms and third-party service providers. FootFinesse is not responsible for the content or privacy practices of these external sites.

Children’s Privacy

FootFinesse is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that your child has provided personal information to FootFinesse without your consent, please contact us immediately.

Compliance and Data Protection

At FootFinesse, we are committed to ensuring compliance with all applicable laws and regulations, including the GDPR and CCPA. We prioritize the protection of your data and privacy.
