Privacy Policy

Welcome to RhythmRise, where the art of dance meets passion and dedication. We are committed to creating a safe and inspiring environment for our students and their families, and protecting your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information so that you can engage with us confidently, knowing your data is in trustworthy hands. Trust and transparency form the foundation of our relationship, and we work diligently to protect it every step of your journey with us.

Information We Collect

At RhythmRise, we value simplicity and transparency regarding data. We only collect the personal information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, and phone number. This data helps us deliver the best level of service and a personalized approach. You may provide this information when signing up for classes, requesting information, or reaching out to our customer support team. We do not engage in invasive data collection methods, nor do we use cookies to track your online behavior unless it’s essential to improve our services. All information gathering is solely focused on enhancing your experience with RhythmRise, making every interaction a seamless one.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to personalize your experience, ensuring that every aspect of your journey with us is handled promptly and with care. This includes class scheduling, event notifications, and updates on upcoming performances. With your consent, we may send updates, promotional offers, and exclusive news about workshops or events that align with your interests. We respect your inbox and only send relevant and meaningful communications. Should you choose, you can always opt out of these messages, receiving only the information that truly matters to you.

How We Share Your Information

At RhythmRise, we value the privacy of your data. We may share your information only with trusted third-party partners who assist us in payment processing, event coordination, and marketing communications. These partners are carefully vetted and are required to protect your information, using it solely for delivering our services. They are prohibited from using your data for any unauthorized actions. In rare cases, we may be required to disclose information by law, such as complying with a court order or participating in an investigation. Should our company undergo significant changes, such as a merger or acquisition, we ensure that your information remains protected and that any transition is handled responsibly.

Security and Data Retention

Ensuring the security of your data is our priority. We use advanced protective measures like data encryption, secure servers, and regular security audits to maintain the integrity and confidentiality of your information. Only authorized personnel, thoroughly trained in data protection, have access to your information. We retain your data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy or as required by law. When your data is no longer needed, we ensure it is securely deleted or anonymized, maintaining your privacy at every step.

Compliance and Data Protection

At RhythmRise, we are committed to ensuring full compliance with all applicable laws and regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We prioritize the protection of your data and privacy, implementing industry-standard security measures to safeguard your personal information.

Data Collection and Usage

We collect, store, and process personal information strictly in accordance with legal requirements and ethical standards. Our data-handling practices are regularly reviewed to maintain compliance with evolving regulations and industry best practices.

Transparency and Accountability

Transparency is at the core of our operations. We clearly outline how your data is collected, used, and stored. Our policies reflect our ongoing efforts to maintain compliance and accountability.

Your Rights and Choices

At RhythmRise, we want you to have complete control over your information. You have the right to access, update, or delete your data at any time. We have made it easy for you to manage your information through your account settings, and if you need assistance, our Privacy Team is always ready to help. Simply contact us at rhythmrise@gmail.com, and we will promptly address your request. You can also adjust your communication preferences, such as opting out of promotional emails, via the links provided in our messages. This ensures that you receive only the information that matters to you, giving you full control over your interaction with us.

Children's Privacy

RhythmRise's services are intended for individuals of all ages, including children, and we understand the importance of protecting children's privacy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that we have accidentally collected information from a minor, we will immediately take steps to remove it from our system. Our commitment to safety and protection extends to all users, and we appreciate your assistance in upholding these standards. If you believe that a child’s information may have been collected, please contact us so we can take the necessary actions to ensure compliance with all relevant privacy laws.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

RhythmRise is constantly evolving, and we may make changes to this Privacy Policy to reflect updates in our services or legal requirements. We are committed to transparency and will always notify you of any updates on our website. We encourage you to periodically review this policy to stay informed about how we protect your data. Continued use of our services after changes are made signifies your acceptance of the updated terms. We are always here to keep you informed, ensuring your trust in RhythmRise remains strong as we grow.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, comments, or suggestions about this Privacy Policy or our data handling practices, please reach out to our Privacy Team. You can contact us at rhythmrise@gmail.com, and we will respond as quickly as possible. We are dedicated to ensuring that your experience with RhythmRise is safe, comfortable, and brings joy to everyone involved. Your feedback helps us improve and ensures the best possible experience for all our students and their families.
