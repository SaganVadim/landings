Your Privacy Matters at SoireeSpark

We Care About Your Data

At SoireeSpark, we value your privacy as much as we value creating unforgettable events. Whether you're booking a gala, a corporate function, or a private soirée, we handle your personal details with care and respect. Our commitment is to keep your data safe and protected at all times. We don’t just follow privacy standards—we exceed them to ensure your information stays secure.

We also prioritize the privacy of minors. If you're under 18, we ask that you refrain from sharing any personal information with us. If any such data is collected accidentally, we will promptly delete it. Your safety and privacy are our top priorities.

How We Protect Your Data

Your personal information is not just stored—it’s safeguarded with the highest level of care. To ensure its security, we store your information safely and only retain it as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. We assess the sensitivity of your data and take the necessary steps to protect it from unauthorized access.

Here’s how we keep your data safe:

  • Advanced encryption and security protocols to protect your personal data
  • Strict internal policies on access, ensuring only authorized staff can handle your information
  • Ongoing staff training to stay up-to-date on the latest privacy and security practices

Compliance and Data Protection

At SoireeSpark, we are committed to ensuring full compliance with all applicable laws and regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We prioritize the protection of your data and privacy, implementing industry-standard security measures to safeguard your personal information.

Data Collection and Usage

We collect, store, and process personal information strictly in accordance with legal requirements and ethical standards. Our data-handling practices are regularly reviewed to maintain compliance with evolving regulations and industry best practices.

Transparency and Accountability

Transparency is at the core of our operations. We clearly outline how your data is collected, used, and stored. Our policies reflect our ongoing efforts to maintain compliance and accountability.

International Data, Same Protection

SoireeSpark operates globally, which means your data may be processed outside of your country. However, our commitment to protecting your privacy remains unchanged, no matter where your information goes. We follow strict international privacy laws and regulations to ensure your data is handled with care across the world.

Any data shared internationally is treated with the same level of protection as it would be in your home country. Your data’s journey is just as safe as your event’s flawless execution!

Keeping You Informed

Privacy laws and technology are constantly evolving. That’s why we regularly update our privacy practices to align with the latest standards and best practices. If there are significant changes to our privacy policy, we’ll notify you through this page. Stay informed by checking back often.

By continuing to use our services after any updates, you agree to the revised terms. Don’t worry—we’ll make sure any major changes are clearly communicated.

As part of our commitment to compliance, we respect your rights to access, update, and delete your personal information. You can request data modifications or removals by contacting us at soireespark@gmail.com.

Sharing Data Responsibly

SoireeSpark may collaborate with trusted third-party providers to deliver top-notch services, such as payment processing, marketing, and customer support. We ensure that any third-party partners follow our strict privacy standards. Rest assured, we will never sell or rent your personal information.

  • We work only with the most reliable and trustworthy third-party providers
  • Strict agreements to ensure third parties adhere to our privacy policies
  • No selling or renting your personal data—ever

Your Data’s Safety is Key

We treat your personal information with the highest level of protection. We continuously monitor and update our cybersecurity measures to prevent breaches and keep your data safe. If any threats are detected, we act swiftly to resolve them, ensuring your information is always secure.

  • Continuous monitoring of our security systems to prevent any data breaches
  • Regular updates to strengthen our cybersecurity protections
  • Immediate action in response to any detected privacy threats
