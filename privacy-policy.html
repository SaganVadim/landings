Your Privacy Matters at FurFriendship

We Care About Your Data

At FurFriendship, we value your privacy as much as we value your pets. Whether you're shopping for toys, food, or accessories, we handle your personal details with care and respect. Our commitment is to keep your data safe and protected at all times. We don't just follow privacy standards—we exceed them to ensure your information stays secure.

We also prioritize the privacy of minors. If you're under 18, we ask that you refrain from sharing any personal information with us. If any such data is collected accidentally, we will promptly delete it. Your safety and privacy are our top priority.

How We Protect Your Data

Your personal information is not just stored—it's safeguarded with the highest level of care. To ensure its security, we store your information safely and only retain it as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. We assess the sensitivity of your data and take the necessary steps to protect it from unauthorized access.

Here’s how we keep your data safe:

  • Advanced encryption and security protocols to protect your personal data
  • Strict internal policies on access, ensuring only authorized staff can handle your information
  • Ongoing staff training to stay up-to-date on the latest privacy and security practices

International Data, Same Protection

FurFriendship operates globally, which means your data may be processed outside of your country. However, our commitment to protecting your privacy remains unchanged, no matter where your information goes. We follow strict international privacy laws and regulations to ensure your data is handled with care across the world.

Any data shared internationally is treated with the same level of protection as it would be in your home country. Your data’s journey is just as safe as your pet’s next adventure!

Keeping You Informed

Privacy laws and technology are constantly evolving. That’s why we regularly update our privacy practices to align with the latest standards and best practices. If there are significant changes to our privacy policy, we’ll notify you through this page. Stay informed by checking back often.

By continuing to use our services after any updates, you agree to the revised terms. Don’t worry—we’ll make sure any major changes are clearly communicated.

Let’s Chat

Have questions or concerns about how we manage your personal data? We're here to help! Feel free to contact us with any privacy-related inquiries or general feedback. Clear communication is key to building trust, and we're always improving.

We Respect Your Data

At FurFriendship, we believe in using your personal data responsibly. We only collect the information necessary to improve your shopping experience, manage orders, and send you relevant offers. Rest assured, we will never process your personal data without your consent.

You always have control over how much personal data you share with us. If you wish to limit or change the information you provide, just let us know—we’re happy to accommodate.

Sharing Data Responsibly

FurFriendship may collaborate with trusted third-party providers to deliver top-notch services, such as payment processing, marketing, and customer support. We ensure that any third-party partners follow our strict privacy standards. Rest assured, we will never sell or rent your personal information.

  • We work only with the most reliable and trustworthy third-party providers
  • Strict agreements to ensure third parties adhere to our privacy policies
  • No selling or renting your personal data—ever

Your Data’s Safety is Key

We treat your personal information with the highest level of protection. We continuously monitor and update our cybersecurity measures to prevent breaches and keep your data safe. If any threats are detected, we act swiftly to resolve them, ensuring your information is always secure.

  • Continuous monitoring of our security systems to prevent any data breaches
  • Regular updates to strengthen our cybersecurity protections
  • Immediate action in response to any detected privacy threats

Your Rights Matter

At FurFriendship, you're in control. Whether you want to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, we're here to assist you. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights related to your data. Don’t hesitate to contact us if you wish to exercise any of your data rights.

We're committed to ensuring your privacy is respected and your personal data is handled securely. If you have any questions or need assistance, our team is here to help!
