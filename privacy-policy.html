Your Journey Begins with WildEmbrace

Trust and Personalized Travel Planning

At WildEmbrace, we understand that every rescued animal is more than just a case: it's a unique life. We are committed to planning journeys that meet the needs of each animal, protecting their personal information at every step of the process. Whether you're a volunteer, donor, or adopter, we ensure every detail is handled with precision. For us, your trust is fundamental, and your privacy is our highest priority.

Our commitment is to ensure your information is only used to create a safe and healthy future for the animals. We work with transparency, always informing you how your data will be treated. With WildEmbrace, you can trust that you are in good hands to transform lives.

Protection of Your Personal Data

At WildEmbrace, we value the security of your data. We store your information only as long as necessary to manage our rescue activities or as required by law. Our approach includes:

  • Secure storage with cutting-edge technologies.
  • Protection of information during transmission and access.
  • Strict access control and robust internal systems.
  • Ongoing training in security and privacy for our team.
  • Regular improvements to our technology infrastructure to offer maximum protection.

With WildEmbrace, you can contribute to a better future for the animals, knowing that we are dedicated to protecting your data.

Compliance and Data Protection

At WildEmbrace, we are committed to ensuring full compliance with all applicable laws and regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We prioritize the protection of your data and privacy, implementing industry-standard security measures to safeguard your personal information.

Data Collection and Usage

We collect, store, and process personal information strictly in accordance with legal requirements and ethical standards. Our data-handling practices are regularly reviewed to maintain compliance with evolving regulations and industry best practices.

Transparency and Accountability

Transparency is at the core of our operations. We clearly outline how your data is collected, used, and stored. Our policies reflect our ongoing efforts to maintain compliance and accountability.

User Rights and Choices

As part of our commitment to compliance, we respect your rights to access, update, and delete your personal information. You can request data modifications or removals by contacting us at wildembrace@gmail.com.

Privacy and International Respect

We work with people from different parts of the world. This means that, in some situations, your data may be processed outside of your country of origin. However, we ensure the same protective measures are applied globally. We comply with regulations such as the GDPR and other international privacy standards.

We work with trusted partners who share our values and ensure that your data is treated with care and respect.

Privacy Policy in Constant Evolution

At WildEmbrace, we regularly review our privacy policy to keep up with changes in laws and technologies. Any changes will be clearly communicated. Your trust and protection are our top priorities, and we guarantee that you will always be informed about how your information is being treated.

Total Transparency

We want you to have full confidence in our services. Therefore, we are always available to clarify any doubts about our privacy policy or the use of your data. Our team is ready to help at any time.

Email: wildembrace@gmail.com

Collaboration with Trusted Partners

At WildEmbrace, we work with selected partners to provide the best adoption and rescue experiences. We ensure that any sharing of information is done following high privacy standards. We never sell or rent your data to third parties, and any use of information is transparent and secure.

Security and Integrity of Information

We adopt stringent security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access. This includes continuous monitoring and the implementation of advanced solutions. Our commitment to security includes:

  • Constant monitoring of systems and data.
  • Use of state-of-the-art digital security tools.
  • Quick and efficient responses to any detected threats.

With WildEmbrace, your privacy and security are always our priority.

Respect for Your Rights

We understand that you have the right to access, correct, or delete your information at any time. We make it easy for you to exercise these rights so you have full control over your data. If you need help, contact us, and we will be happy to assist you.
