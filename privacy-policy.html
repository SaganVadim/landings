MindMotion Privacy Policy

Welcome to MindMotion, your space for holistic wellness, mindfulness, and intentional movement. Your privacy is our top priority, and we are dedicated to protecting your personal information at every step of your journey. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data to ensure a secure and fulfilling experience.

Our Privacy Promise

At MindMotion, we respect and protect your personal information with the highest standards of security and transparency. We only collect data that is necessary to provide you with a seamless wellness experience. Your trust is our foundation, and we never share your data with third parties without your explicit consent. Our commitment to privacy means that we continuously review and improve our policies to keep your data safe.

What Information We Collect

To enhance your experience and offer personalized guidance, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name, email address, and contact details
  • Account preferences, class attendance, and fitness progress
  • Feedback, reviews, and interactions within our community
  • Device and browsing information to optimize your experience

We collect this information directly from you through account registrations, class sign-ups, and interactions with our platform. Some data may be gathered automatically through cookies and analytics tools to improve your user experience.

How We Use Your Data

Your personal information is used to:

  • Provide personalized wellness plans and class recommendations
  • Enhance your learning experience with tailored content
  • Improve our platform’s usability and functionality
  • Communicate important updates, promotions, and support messages

We strictly use your data to enhance your yoga and fitness journey. It is never sold or shared for unrelated commercial purposes.

Data Security

MindMotion employs advanced security protocols to protect your information from unauthorized access, breaches, or misuse. Our platform uses encryption, secure servers, and regular audits to uphold the strongest security measures. We also provide you with tools to manage and control your privacy settings.

Third-Party Services

To enhance our services, we may collaborate with trusted third-party providers, such as payment processors and analytics tools. These partners are contractually bound to our privacy standards and cannot use your data for any purpose beyond our agreed services.

Your Rights

As a valued user, you have the right to:

  • Access, update, or delete your personal data
  • Request details about how your data is used
  • Manage communication preferences and opt-out options

You can exercise these rights by contacting our support team at mindmotion@gmail.com.

Compliance and Data Protection

At MindMotion, we are committed to ensuring full compliance with all applicable laws and regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We prioritize the protection of your data and privacy, implementing industry-standard security measures to safeguard your personal information. Regular audits and assessments help us maintain the highest levels of compliance.

Data Collection and Usage

We collect, store, and process personal information strictly in accordance with legal requirements and ethical standards. Our data-handling practices are regularly reviewed to maintain compliance with evolving regulations and industry best practices. We are dedicated to maintaining the integrity of our data policies, ensuring your information is used only for intended purposes.

Transparency and Accountability

Transparency is at the core of our operations. We clearly outline how your data is collected, used, and stored. Our policies reflect our ongoing efforts to maintain compliance and accountability. We are always available to answer any questions about our data practices.

User Rights and Choices

As part of our commitment to compliance, we respect your rights to access, update, and delete your personal information. You can request data modifications or removals by contacting us at mindmotion@gmail.com. Additionally, you can manage your data preferences through your account settings.

Changes to This Policy

Our Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in our practices, services, or legal requirements. We encourage you to review this page regularly to stay informed about how we protect your data. Significant updates will be communicated directly to our users.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about our Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at mindmotion@gmail.com. Your privacy and trust are important to us, and we are here to address any inquiries you may have.
