RusticRoots Privacy Policy

Welcome to RusticRoots, your trusted source for farm-fresh products. We respect your privacy and are committed to ensuring that your experience with us is secure and transparent. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information as you engage with our platform and services. Whether you're selecting farm-fresh produce or exploring our offerings, you can trust that your data is safeguarded at all times.

Compliance and Data Protection

At RusticRoots, we are committed to ensuring full compliance with all applicable laws and regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We prioritize the protection of your data and privacy, implementing industry-standard security measures to safeguard your personal information.

Data Collection and Usage

We collect, store, and process personal information strictly in accordance with legal requirements and ethical standards. Our data-handling practices are regularly reviewed to maintain compliance with evolving regulations and industry best practices.

Transparency and Accountability

Transparency is at the core of our operations. We clearly outline how your data is collected, used, and stored. Our policies reflect our ongoing efforts to maintain compliance and accountability.

User Rights and Choices

As part of our commitment to compliance, we respect your rights to access, update, and delete your personal information. You can request data modifications or removals by contacting us at RusticRoots@gmail.com.

Protecting Your Data

RusticRoots employs advanced security measures to protect your information. Any data you provide is securely encrypted and stored to prevent unauthorized access. Our platform undergoes regular security audits to ensure your data remains safe.

Engagement with Third Parties

RusticRoots partners only with trusted third-party providers, such as payment processors and hosting services, ensuring they adhere to strict data protection standards. We never share your personal data beyond what is necessary to deliver our services.

Privacy for Children

RusticRoots' services are intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If such data is identified, we take immediate steps to delete it.

Changes to This Policy

RusticRoots reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy as needed. Any changes will be clearly communicated on our website, and we encourage users to review our policy periodically to stay informed.

Get in Touch

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please contact us at RusticRoots@gmail.com. Our support team is available to assist you.
