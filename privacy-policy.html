IdiomaIQ Privacy Policy

Welcome to IdiomaIQ, your trusted partner in mastering foreign languages. We value your privacy and are dedicated to ensuring that your learning journey with us is both safe and secure. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information as you engage with our platform and services. Whether you're practicing your conversation skills or accessing our interactive learning tools, you can trust that your personal data is protected at all times. Our mission is to support your language learning while safeguarding your privacy every step of the way.

Our Dedication to Privacy

At IdiomaIQ, protecting your personal data is our top priority. We employ industry-leading encryption and security practices to ensure your information is always safe. We collect only the data necessary to deliver our services effectively, minimizing the amount of information required. Our platform does not rely on invasive tracking technologies like cookies without your consent. Additionally, we regularly update our security measures to comply with the latest legal standards, ensuring that your learning environment is transparent and secure. Your trust is essential to us, and we are committed to continuously improving how we protect your information.

Learning Anonymously

At IdiomaIQ, we respect your right to privacy. You can explore our courses, practice tools, and language resources without providing any personal details. There's no obligation to create an account or share personal information to browse our website. We believe that your learning experience should be private and free from unnecessary data collection. By offering anonymous browsing, we ensure that you can confidently explore our platform without compromising your personal information. This freedom allows you to focus on your language goals without concerns about privacy.

Confidentiality Commitment

At IdiomaIQ, we value your trust and are dedicated to keeping your personal data confidential. We do not share your information with third parties unless legally required or with your explicit consent. You can rest assured that your data will never be sold or used for marketing purposes without your knowledge. We maintain strict internal policies and work only with trusted partners who uphold the same high standards of confidentiality. Transparency is central to our commitment, and we will promptly inform you of any policy changes to ensure you remain in control of your data.

Protecting Your Data

IdiomaIQ employs advanced security measures to safeguard your information. Any data you provide is encrypted and stored securely to prevent unauthorized access. Our platform undergoes regular security audits to identify and address vulnerabilities, ensuring your data remains safe. Whether you're creating an account, subscribing to a course, or using our interactive tools, you can trust that we prioritize the security of your personal information. Our proactive approach to data protection reflects our commitment to providing a safe and secure learning environment.

Engagement with Third Parties

IdiomaIQ only works with trusted third-party providers to support your learning experience. These partners include payment processors, hosting services, and customer support platforms. We carefully vet each partner to ensure they comply with strict data protection standards. Additionally, we maintain detailed agreements with these providers to guarantee the confidentiality and security of your information. Your data will never be shared with external services beyond what is strictly necessary to deliver our offerings. This commitment ensures that your personal information is handled responsibly and securely at all times.

Privacy for Children

IdiomaIQ’s services are designed for learners aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If we discover that a child has provided personal data, we will take immediate steps to delete this information and prevent future collection. Protecting minors is a key priority for us, and we comply with all relevant laws and regulations regarding children’s privacy. By ensuring that our platform is geared toward adults, we maintain a secure and appropriate environment for all learners.

Changes to This Policy

IdiomaIQ reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy as needed to reflect changes in our practices or compliance with legal requirements. Any updates will be communicated clearly on our website, along with an explanation of the changes. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we handle your data. By continuing to use our services after updates, you indicate your acceptance of the new terms. Transparency and clear communication are integral to our approach, ensuring that you always understand how your information is managed.

Get in Touch

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please feel free to contact us at IdiomaIQ@gmail.com. Our dedicated support team is ready to assist you and address any concerns you may have. Your feedback is important to us as we strive to provide the best possible experience for our learners. Whether you need clarification on our privacy practices or support with your account, we are here to help.

Policy on Data Retention

IdiomaIQ retains personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services effectively. Any data collected during your interaction with our platform is securely deleted when it is no longer needed. This ensures that we minimize data storage and reduce potential risks to your privacy. Our data retention practices align with our larger commitment to protecting your information and respecting your right to privacy. By collecting only the data essential for your learning experience, we strive to maintain a secure and user-friendly environment.

Rights of Users

At IdiomaIQ, we respect your privacy rights. You have the right to inquire about how your data is handled and to request clarification on our privacy practices at any time. If you have concerns about your information, we encourage you to contact us, and we will respond promptly. Your right to data protection is fundamental to our values, and we are committed to upholding the highest standards in privacy and security. We want you to feel confident and secure as you focus on your language-learning goals.
