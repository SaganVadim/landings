Privacy Policy

Welcome to VigilanceTech! We’re delighted to be your trusted partner for video surveillance system installations. Your privacy is of utmost importance to us, and we are committed to safeguarding your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, protect, and handle your data in connection with our services.

Information We Collect

At VigilanceTech, we respect your privacy and collect personal information only when you provide it willingly. This typically occurs when you interact with us via our website, email, or other communication channels. The personal data we may collect includes your name, contact information, and any other relevant details you share with us. We do not utilize cookies or tracking technologies to monitor your online activities. Transparency is our priority; your information will only be used for the purposes outlined in this policy.

How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is essential for us to deliver and enhance our installation services. It enables us to respond to your inquiries, process your requests, and manage our communication effectively. For instance, we may use your contact details to send important updates about our services or respond to your questions. With your consent, we may also send newsletters or promotional offers related to our video surveillance products. You have the option to opt out of these communications at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in our emails or contacting us directly at VigilanceTech@gmail.com.

How We Share Your Information

Your privacy is our priority; we do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. However, we may share your data with trusted service providers who assist us in delivering our services, such as payment processing or customer support. These partners are required to maintain the confidentiality of your information and use it solely for their designated purposes. We may also disclose your information if required by law, in response to legal requests, or to protect the rights and safety of our company and others.

Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously and implement robust measures to protect it from unauthorized access or disclosure. Our security practices include encryption, secure servers, and regular audits. While we strive to maintain the highest security standards, please be aware that no system is entirely infallible. Exercise caution when transmitting sensitive information online, as any data you share is at your own risk.

Your Choices

You have the right to review, update, or delete your personal information at any time. If you wish to make any changes or request deletion of your data, please contact us at VigilanceTech@gmail.com, and we will assist you promptly. If you no longer wish to receive marketing communications, you can opt out via the unsubscribe link in our emails or by contacting us directly.

Children's Privacy

VigilanceTech services are intended for a general audience and are not specifically targeted towards children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under this age. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will take immediate steps to delete that information. Protecting children’s privacy is important to us, and we adhere to relevant regulations to safeguard younger users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When significant updates are made, we will notify you through email or by posting a notice on our website prior to the changes taking effect. We encourage you to review this policy regularly to stay informed about how we protect your information.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at VigilanceTech@gmail.com. Our team is here to assist you and will address your concerns promptly. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we are committed to ensuring a secure and satisfactory experience with VigilanceTech.
