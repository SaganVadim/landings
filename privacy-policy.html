FlameFeast Privacy Policy

At FlameFeast, we’re not just about crafting delicious pizzas; we’re also dedicated to ensuring your experience is as secure and private as it is satisfying. We understand that privacy matters, and we’re committed to safeguarding your personal information. While you explore our menu and place your orders, rest assured that your data is handled with the same care and attention as our finest ingredients.

Our Commitment to Privacy

At FlameFeast, we believe in keeping things simple—both in our recipes and our approach to your privacy. We only collect the necessary information to process your orders and enhance your experience. No excessive tracking, no hidden data collection—just honest and transparent practices built with your trust in mind.

Anonymous Browsing

Enjoy browsing our menu without worrying about unnecessary data collection. You can explore our offerings freely, without the need to create an account or share personal details unless placing an order.

Confidentiality Commitment

Your personal data stays confidential. If we collect any information for order processing, it is securely stored and never shared or sold. Your details are safe with us—just like our secret sauce recipes.

Security Measures

We take security seriously. Our website and payment processing systems use advanced encryption and security protocols to ensure that your data remains protected at all times.

Third-Party Services

FlameFeast works with trusted partners for payment processing and order fulfillment. These third parties are carefully selected and meet strict privacy and security standards to ensure the protection of your data.

Children's Privacy

FlameFeast is intended for customers aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If any such data is inadvertently collected, it will be promptly deleted.

Policy Updates

Our Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page to keep you informed about how we protect your data.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about our privacy practices, please contact us at FlameFeast@gmail.com. We’re here to ensure your privacy concerns are addressed promptly.

Data Retention Policy

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to fulfill your orders and comply with legal obligations. After this period, any retained data is securely deleted.

User Rights

As a FlameFeast customer, you have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal data. Contact us if you wish to make any changes to your information.

Compliance and Data Protection

At FlameFeast, we are committed to ensuring full compliance with all applicable laws and regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We prioritize the protection of your data and privacy, implementing industry-standard security measures to safeguard your personal information.

Data Collection and Usage

We collect, store, and process personal information strictly in accordance with legal requirements and ethical standards. Our data-handling practices are regularly reviewed to maintain compliance with evolving regulations and industry best practices.

Transparency and Accountability

Transparency is at the core of our operations. We clearly outline how your data is collected, used, and stored. Our policies reflect our ongoing efforts to maintain compliance and accountability.

User Rights and Choices

As part of our commitment to compliance, we respect your rights to access, update, and delete your personal information. You can request data modifications or removals by contacting us at FlameFeast@gmail.com.
