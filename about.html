Top Tips for a Stellar Party

Planning a party can feel like a big adventure, but we’ve got your back! Our tips slider is packed with clever ideas to make your celebration shine, whether you’re working with us or going DIY. From picking the right time of day to keeping little guests engaged, these nuggets of wisdom come straight from our years of party-planning expertise. Slide through to learn how to set the stage for a day your child will never forget—because a little know-how goes a long way!

Timing is Everything

Plan your party for late morning or early afternoon when kids are full of energy but not too tired. A 2-3 hour window keeps the fun flowing without wearing anyone out!

Keep It Interactive

Kids love to move! Add games, scavenger hunts, or a simple dance-off to keep them engaged and laughing throughout the party.

Snacks That Wow

Go beyond chips—think mini sandwiches shaped like stars or fruit wands with a sprinkle of edible glitter. Tasty and fun keep everyone happy!
