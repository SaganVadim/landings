Common Questions

Curious about us? Find answers to your questions about Tegumenia’s crafted treasures.

What are your operating hours?

Our boutique welcomes you from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. For special appointments, we’re happy to accommodate upon request.

Can I customize a leather item?

Certainly. Our artisans bring your vision to life, creating personalized pieces that reflect your style. Contact us for custom orders.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, our crafted goods travel the world to reach you, wherever you may be, delivered with the same care we put into creating them.

Do you offer gift wrapping services?

Indeed. Every gift from Tegumenia is elegantly wrapped to match the sophistication of the treasures inside.

Where do you source your materials?

We partner with tanneries that uphold timeless traditions, using hides chosen for both their quality and their sustainable origins.

Can I schedule a private consultation?

Yes, we delight in offering private consultations to help you find or create a piece that speaks to your unique taste.

Is your leather sourced ethically?

Absolutely. We’re committed to ethical practices, working only with suppliers who value humane and sustainable methods.

Where can I visit your store?

Our flagship boutique is located in the heart of the city, where leather artistry comes alive. Come and experience Tegumenia firsthand.
