Discover the Ease of Grocery Delivery

How do I place an order with PantryPilot?

Just visit our website, pick your groceries, and choose a delivery time that works for you. We'll even send you a text when your order is on its way. Need help? Our team is just a click away.

What’s your delivery policy?

We offer flexible delivery options to fit your schedule. Choose a delivery window that works best for you, and receive a text when your order is on its way. If you need to make any changes to your order, you can do so up to 24 hours before delivery. And if you're not available to receive your order, we'll work with you to schedule a redelivery at no extra cost. Visit our delivery page for all the details.

Can I set up recurring deliveries?

Absolutely! With our 'Repeat Delivery' feature, you can schedule regular deliveries of your favorite items and never run out. This feature is perfect for households with pets, families with young children, or simply for those who want to keep their pantry stocked with their favorite snacks. Just select the items you want to receive on a recurring basis, choose your preferred delivery frequency, and we'll take care of the rest.

Do you stock specialty products?

Yes! From organic to gluten-free and international products, PantryPilot offers a diverse selection to meet your needs. We also carry a wide variety of specialty items, such as vegan, dairy-free, kosher, and halal products. Our product selection is constantly evolving, so if there's something you can't find on our website, please don't hesitate to reach out to our customer service team. We're always happy to help you find what you need.
