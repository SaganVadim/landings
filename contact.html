IdiomaIQ FAQs

Answers to Your Language Learning Questions

What languages can I learn?

We offer an extensive range of over 20 languages for you to choose from. This includes widely spoken languages like English, Spanish, French, and Mandarin, as well as unique and regional dialects. Whether you're looking to expand your professional opportunities, prepare for travel, or simply explore a new culture, you'll find a language that suits your goals.

Are your lessons online?

Yes, all our lessons are conducted online to provide maximum convenience and accessibility. Through our interactive virtual classrooms, you’ll engage directly with professional tutors in real-time, participate in group discussions, and access a wealth of learning materials. You can study from anywhere in the world, ensuring flexibility to fit into your busy schedule.

How quickly can I learn?

The speed at which you learn depends on several factors, such as your previous experience, dedication, and the amount of time you commit. Most students achieve basic conversational skills within a few months with regular practice. However, for those seeking fluency, we provide advanced courses and comprehensive resources to guide you through your learning journey step by step.

Do you provide certifications?

Yes, we award certificates upon successful completion of your course. These certificates are recognized by various institutions and employers, making them an excellent addition to your professional profile. They not only validate your language proficiency but also demonstrate your commitment to personal and professional growth.

Can I customize my course?

Absolutely. Our courses are designed to be highly adaptable to meet your individual needs. Whether you're focusing on business communication, academic preparation, or casual conversation, we create personalized lesson plans tailored to your specific goals and preferred learning style. This ensures an effective and enjoyable learning experience.
