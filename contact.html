Textile Care Made Simple

Keep your TextileTreasure pieces fresh and fabulous with these easy tips. We’re here to help you enjoy your fabrics longer.

Washing Wisdom

Use cold water and a gentle cycle for bedding—your fabrics will thank you with lasting softness. Hot water can break down fibers over time, causing fading and shrinkage, especially in delicate materials. By opting for a mild, fragrance-free detergent and avoiding harsh bleach, you not only protect the colors and textures of your linens but also extend their usable life. Washing less frequently when possible also helps reduce fabric wear and supports a more sustainable routine.

Drying Done Right

Air-dry towels when possible, or tumble low to preserve their fluffiness and absorbency. Excessive heat from dryers can stiffen the loops of the fabric, making towels feel rough and reducing their ability to soak up moisture. If you use a dryer, add wool dryer balls to soften fibers naturally and reduce static. Hang-drying in indirect sunlight is ideal, as direct exposure may bleach colors or weaken fabric over time. Proper drying keeps your towels plush, breathable, and luxurious.

Stain SOS

Blot—don’t rub—spills with a mild detergent. Quick action keeps your textiles pristine. Rubbing can grind the stain deeper into the fibers and distort the weave, especially in fine fabrics like linen or silk. Instead, press gently with a clean cloth, working from the outside of the stain inward to prevent spreading. For tougher spots, use a mixture of water and white vinegar or baking soda paste, depending on the fabric type. Always test stain removers on a hidden section first. Acting fast and treating gently ensures your textiles stay fresh and flawless.
